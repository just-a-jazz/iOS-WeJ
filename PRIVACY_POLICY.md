# WeJ Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WeJ ("we", "our", "us") handles information when you use the WeJ mobile app.

## Summary

- WeJ is designed to let users create and join shared music parties.
- This release uses Apple Music integration.
- We do not use third-party advertising SDKs.
- We do not use third-party analytics SDKs.
- We do not sell personal information.

## Information We Access

WeJ may request access to:

- Apple Music account authorization
- Apple Music and local media library data (for music playback and library browsing)
- Local network access (for discovering and joining nearby parties)

## How Information Is Used

We use accessed information only to provide core app functionality, including:

- Authenticating Apple Music access
- Searching and loading music content
- Playing tracks
- Discovering and connecting to nearby parties on local network

## Data Collection and Retention

- WeJ does not intentionally collect personal data for advertising.
- WeJ does not intentionally profile users.
- WeJ does not intentionally sell user data.
- Any network requests needed to serve real-time app functionality are processed only as required to complete those requests.

If this changes in a future release, this policy will be updated before release.

## Third-Party Services

WeJ relies on Apple frameworks and Apple Music services to provide music features. Use of Apple services is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

- Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Apple Music: https://www.apple.com/apple-music/

## Children's Privacy

WeJ is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information used by the app. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

## International Users

If you use WeJ outside your home country, your information may be processed in countries where the service providers operate.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
